Our story

A thoughtful approach to furnishing.

Oak & Ember Living began with a simple idea: choosing furniture should feel inspiring, not overwhelming. We bring together practical pieces, warm materials and versatile designs to help people create spaces they love coming back to.

Whether you are furnishing your first apartment, refreshing a family home or creating a welcoming workspace, we aim to make the process clear, personal and enjoyable.
